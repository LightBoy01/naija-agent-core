Month 0 — Today

Why 95% margin holds at scale

The CapabilityRouter's free-tier arbitrage (Alibaba Qwen, DeepSeek free tier, Groq free tier, Cloudflare Workers AI) means the majority of AI calls cost zero. Context caching eliminates ~90% of system-prompt tokens for repeat tenant interactions. Per-message micro-billing at 3,300 kobo (₦33) per reply is priced at a 40x markup over actual inference cost. The infrastructure is fixed-cost — adding tenant #201 costs nothing in server fees, only the marginal AI inference. This is the leverage dimension compounding: a 1% increase in free-tier routing is pure margin.

The Moats (Why This Wins)

DATA Fraud Network Moat

GlobalFraudGuard at 150+ tenants has seen 40,000+ unique phone numbers. The G2 consensus rule (2+ orgs must report) means the blacklist has near-zero false positives. A new competitor starting from zero has no fraud data. The first fraudulent transaction they process is one NaijaAgent's network already blocked. This moat compounds daily — every new tenant adds a reporter, every blocked scammer improves precision.

SOCIAL Referral Graph Moat

The 30% rev-share with 365-day tracking creates a financial incentive graph. Partners who referred 20+ businesses are earning ₦45,000+/mo in passive commission. They won't leave — and they actively recruit to protect their income stream. The referral graph maps directly onto Nigerian trade associations, market unions, and business networks. A competitor can offer the same product at half price, but they can't replicate the ₦2.4M+ in existing partner earnings.

SWITCHING Per-Tenant AI Tuning Moat

Every tenant's AI agent learns over time: product knowledge base, customer interaction patterns, pricing logic, business-specific policies. After 6 months, a merchant's Zynux bot has processed 5,000+ customer interactions and developed a tuned understanding of that specific business. The context cache has their unique system prompt permanently cached. Migrating to a competitor means starting from a blank AI — months of accumulated customer interaction intelligence, lost. The switching cost isn't financial; it's the AI getting dumber for weeks.

COST Sovereign Infrastructure Moat

At €38/mo for 200 tenants (€0.19/tenant/month), the infrastructure cost is two orders of magnitude below any VC-funded competitor. A competitor on AWS/Azure/Google Cloud would pay €3-8/tenant/month for equivalent compute. NaijaAgent can price at ₦15,000/mo and maintain 95% margins; a cloud-dependent competitor would need ₦45,000-60,000/mo for the same margin. This isn't a temporary advantage — it's structural, based on the self-hosting architecture and free-tier AI arbitrage that gets better as more providers offer free tiers.
